Why AJH Trust

Serving Srirangam with care and compassion

Free Food, Every Day

We serve free meals daily to at least 15 people who need it most.

Monthly Medical Camp

Health check-ups and medical help for families in the community, every month.

Village Relief

We reach out to nearby villages with essential relief support when families need it.

Blankets for the Homeless

We give blankets to destitute people sleeping on the roadside, so no one faces the cold alone.

Scheme Awareness & Guidance

We help people understand government schemes and guide them on housing support.
