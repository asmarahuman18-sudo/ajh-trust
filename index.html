Our Services

Our services are: daily free food for at least 15 people, a monthly medical camp, village relief, blankets for destitute people on the roadside, housing scheme guidance, awareness of government schemes, medical financial aid, marriage aid and financial aid.

Call or WhatsApp us on 6383507469 and tell us about the need, such as medical, marriage or financial aid. Our team will speak with you about the next steps.

We are based in Mutharasanallur, Trichy, and our work focuses on Mutharasanallur, Trichy and nearby villages.
